Meet the CEO - Harriet Taylor

Harriet Taylor, the visionary behind Tc bridals, is passionate about helping brides look and feel their best. With over 10 years of experience in the bridal fashion industry, she brings elegance, comfort, and confidence to every piece curated for our store.

Taylor believes in celebrating individuality and ensuring every bride finds the perfect accessories to complement her dream day. From humble beginnings to a well-known name in Kampala’s bridal scene, her journey inspires excellence, grace, and beauty.
